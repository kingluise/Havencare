Welcome to Haven

Support built around the person, not just the task.

Haven Care Services is a Nigeria-based home care organisation in Lagos. We believe good care begins by listening: understanding each person's routines, preferences, abilities and goals, then working with them and their families to plan thoughtful support.

Our aim is to help people feel respected, comfortable and supported while maintaining as much independence as possible.

  • Respect for each person's dignity and choices
  • Individualised support shaped around daily needs
  • Clear communication with clients and families
